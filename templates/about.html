About D Cool Cafe

Brewed with Love in Burla

From our cozy corner above Goocool in Burla Daily Market, we set out with one simple dream: to create a space that feels like home.

The Beginning

A Vintage Haven for Friends & Foodies

Nestled in the bustling market of Burla, D Cool Cafe was born out of a desire to create a calm, warm, and vintage sanctuary where college students from VSSUT and VIMSAR, local families, and travelers could slow down, enjoy exceptional food, and connect.

Our philosophy is immortalized in our Instagram motto: "A vintage vibe, delicious food, friendly service, passionate team & Fun Experience." Every cup of cold coffee we froth, every pizza crust we bake, and every playlist we curate is guided by this promise.
